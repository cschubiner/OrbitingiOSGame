# Star Stream privacy policy

Updated October 15, 2026.

Star Stream: Orbit Adventure stores scores, player name, stars, unlocks, missions, sound preferences, and verified purchase identifiers on your device. The game does not require a developer account or send this information to a developer-operated server. It has no advertising, tracking, or analytics service.

Optional in-app purchases use Apple StoreKit. Apple processes payment and Apple Account information under its own privacy policy. The game verifies transactions on-device and saves delivery identifiers locally to prevent duplicate grants. Permanent perks can be restored using the same Apple Account. Delivered consumable star packs are not restored.

Gameplay works offline. Apple handles App Store ratings. If you contact support, the information you send is used to respond to your request.

Deleting the app removes local saved progress and the local star balance, subject to device backups you manage through Apple.

For questions, open an issue at https://github.com/cschubiner/OrbitingiOSGame/issues. Do not include private information in a public issue.
