# Star Stream privacy policy

Updated October 15, 2026.

Star Stream: Orbit Adventure stores your scores, player name, earned stars, unlocks, missions, and sound preferences on your device. The game does not require an account and does not send this information to the developer. It has no advertising, tracking, analytics service, or in-app purchases.

Gameplay works offline. Apple handles any App Store rating you choose to leave under Apple's privacy policy. If you contact support, the information you send is used to respond to your request.

Deleting the app removes its local saved progress, subject to any device backups you manage through Apple.

For questions, open an issue at https://github.com/cschubiner/OrbitingiOSGame/issues. Do not include private information in a public issue.
