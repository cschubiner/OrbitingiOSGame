# Star Stream support

Swipe toward the next planet to launch your rocket. Orbit planets, collect stars, avoid asteroids, and reach new galaxies before the battery runs out. Complete missions to earn bonus stars and spend earned stars in the shop on upgrades, perks, ships, and trails.

The green pause button opens Pause, Retry, Resume, and Quit. The speaker button controls game audio. Scores and progress are saved on your device; an internet connection is not required to play.

If something goes wrong, open an issue at https://github.com/cschubiner/OrbitingiOSGame/issues with your device model, iOS version, app version, and reproduction steps. Please do not include private information. Removing the app deletes its local progress.

Optional purchases: The Stars shop offers five paid star packs and permanent Double Stars and Pink Stars perks. Prices are localized by the App Store. Core gameplay and earned-star unlocks remain available offline. Purchases require an App Store connection. Use Restore Purchases in the shop to restore permanent perks purchased with the same Apple Account. Delivered consumable packs cannot be restored. Keep the app installed to preserve its local balance.
